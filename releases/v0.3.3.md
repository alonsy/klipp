> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Signed builds are coming.

### New

- **Discord on its own track** (experimental, off until you turn it on in Settings → Audio or in setup). Discord voice chat goes on a Chat track and everything else on Game, so you can turn your squad down in the editor or leave them out when you share. Klipp follows Discord as it starts, quits and updates itself, and still keeps its own save sound out of your clips. **Check levels** shows each track live, so you can see Discord lands on Chat. It works with the Discord app, not Discord in a browser, and not while an audio mixer like SteelSeries Sonar or Voicemeeter is installed (Sonar's own Chat track already does this).
- **Without voices**, in the editor's Export panel: leaves the Chat and Mic tracks out of what you export, for clips you post publicly. It shows when a clip has voice tracks to leave out.
