**Download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.3.2/Klipp-Setup.exe), run it, done.** Already have Klipp? It offers this update itself.

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Signed builds are coming.

### Fixed

- **Clips no longer freeze after your screen sleeps.** After the screen went to sleep or was switched off and on, Klipp could keep recording the last picture it had seen until it was restarted, sometimes for hours: clips had the game's sound over a still screen. Klipp now notices when Windows ends its view of the screen, or when the picture stops changing while you're using the PC, and starts it again within seconds without losing what it has buffered. If it still can't see the screen, the sidebar says so.
- **A save key another app holds no longer looks like it works.** The sidebar says when another app uses your key, and Klipp takes the key as soon as it's free, without a restart.
- **"Can't keep up" only shows when it's true.** A one-off hiccup, like the first seconds after Klipp starts, no longer sets it off. Klipp's video work also gets a higher priority on the graphics card, so a game that keeps the card busy is less likely to make clips stutter.
- A clip saved right after changing a setting gets its thumbnail.

### New

- **Test your save key.** Setup and Settings → Recording have a **Test** button: press your key and Klipp tells you it works, that another app uses it, or that nothing arrived (on many laptops the F keys need Fn).
- **F8 is the default save key on new installs, Alt+F8 on laptops.** Alt+F10 is also NVIDIA's Instant Replay key and was often taken. Your current key stays.
- Uninstalling Klipp opens a short page that asks what made you leave. Answering is optional.
