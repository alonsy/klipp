Otherwise download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.4.1-test.0/Klipp-Setup.exe) and run it over your Klipp. Not a normal release; most people should stay on [the latest release](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/latest).

This is Klipp 0.4.0, the release for everyone, as a test build: the same program on the test channel, so the next test builds start from here.

### New since 0.4.0-test.3

- **Share a clip as a link.** Sign in at Settings → Account (with Steam or Discord, on klipp.dev). Then open a clip, press Export and **Share as a link**: Klipp uploads it to your Klipp account and copies a link that plays right in Discord. Only people with the link can watch it until you make it public. A free account holds 2 GB, and its shared videos start with a small Klipp tab for a moment.
- **Your clips on klipp.dev.** What you shared is in your library there, with a profile, a feed, likes, comments and follows. Clips get there from Klipp on your PC and no other way.
- **The app works without an account,** exactly as before. Nothing is uploaded unless you share it.

### Testing

- **Sign in and share a clip as a link.** Did the link copy, and does it play when you paste it in Discord?
- **On klipp.dev:** give the clip a title, make it public, and look at your profile and the feed. What was confusing?
- **Sign out** in Settings → Account, then try to share: does Klipp ask you to sign in again?
