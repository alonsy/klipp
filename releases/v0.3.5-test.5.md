**Test build.** For testers: if you have 0.3.5-test.3 or newer, Klipp installs this one by itself between games; with test.1 or test.2, Klipp offers it. Otherwise download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.3.5-test.5/Klipp-Setup.exe) and run it over your Klipp. Not a normal release yet; most people should stay on [the latest release](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/latest).

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New

- **Klipp tests your PC.** Setup records a few seconds at several settings and draws what Klipp will take of your RAM, graphics card and CPU, and how big clips get, changing as you change the settings. After this update, the test shows once by itself. The same drawings are in Settings → Video, with Test again.
- **Squeeze space.** Settings → Storage → Squeeze space: make your clips smaller by re-encoding them on the graphics card, at Low, Mid, High or Ultra. Each level says how much space it frees and how much quality it gives up. Only clips Klipp saved are squeezed. Every audio track and marker stays; favorites are left out unless you say otherwise. It replaces the clips, so it asks first, and a clip can only be squeezed twice, each time harder.
- **Video presets.** Settings → Video: Performance+, Performance, Normal and High, picked for your PC, or Custom. Every setting is still there, with a wider choice of bitrates and the encoder's speed.
- **Settings laid out again.** Recording has manual clipping, auto-clips and the save sounds and marks in one place. Audio is a plain output and microphone, Sonar's tracks when Sonar is found, and Multi Device Track Mode for sound cards and audio software. Auto-clips shows a box per game. Advanced has updates, logs and the folders.
- **Manual clipping can be off.** Then Klipp records only while a game it auto-clips is running.
- **Delete old clips by themselves.** Settings → Storage: clips older than a number of days, or the oldest ones once clips take more than a size. Only clips Klipp saved are deleted, and never favorites. Off unless you turn it on.
- **Updates install by themselves** when you're not playing, or when Klipp starts. On by default, for normal releases too. Settings → Advanced.

### Changed

- The save sound for manual clips and for auto-clips has a switch each, and the volume a mute button.
- Clips play when opened, and loop starts off. The Playback page is gone; the editor's keys are under About → See shortcuts.
- Logs are sent to the developer by default on test builds only.
