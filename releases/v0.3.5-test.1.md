**Test build.** For testers: download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.3.5-test.1/Klipp-Setup.exe) and run it over your Klipp. Not a normal release yet; most people should stay on [the latest release](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/latest).

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Testing

- **Smooth clips while a game keeps the graphics card busy.** On some PCs a game at full load left Klipp waiting on the card for nearly every frame, so clips stuttered even at 60 fps. Encoding now runs on its own, with a few frames queued, so a wait on the card no longer holds up the next frame. The log shows more about where time goes, to confirm it on more PCs.

### New

- **Test channel.** Settings → About → Updates: get test builds as they come out, or go back to normal releases with one click. This build starts on test builds.
- **Send the log to the developer** (test builds only, on by default, can be turned off there). Every 15 minutes the new part of Klipp's log goes to klipp.dev, so problems can be looked into without asking you for it. Your Windows user name is left out; no clips, pictures or settings are sent.
