**Test build.** For testers: Klipp installs this one by itself between games, or offers it on older test builds. Otherwise download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.4.0-test.1/Klipp-Setup.exe) and run it over your Klipp. Not a normal release yet; most people should stay on [the latest release](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/latest).

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New

- **A new look.** Klipp is calmer and quicker to read: fewer words and buttons, and smooth movement between pages.
- **Fortnite auto-clips.** Eliminations, knocks and Victory Royales become clips by themselves. Fortnite shares nothing while a match is on, so Klipp reads your eliminations counter off the screen it records anyway; nothing touches the game. It works at 16:9, fullscreen, with the default HUD. Other screens and HUD settings get few or no clips so far. Settings → Auto-clips has its switch.
- **It stops itself if it reads wrong.** If clips come too fast, or the counter does something it can't, Fortnite's auto-clips pause for 5 minutes, and until you leave Fortnite if it happens again. Hover over Klipp's icon next to the clock to see it. Nothing counts until the counter has shown 0, so if you start Klipp in the middle of a match, clips start with the next one.
- **Say "Klipp that".** Saves a clip by voice. Off until you turn it on in Settings → Recording, where you can teach Klipp how you say it. It listens on your PC only; nothing is kept and nothing is sent. A few clips a minute at most, however often it hears the phrase.
- **Content Creator Mode (experimental).** Records your camera with every clip, and turns clips into shorts.
- **Playback speed in the editor,** from 0.25x to 2x.

### Fixed

- **Exports work on AMD and Intel graphics.** Exporting to share or in high quality failed on any PC without an NVIDIA card.
- **A microphone that recorded silence.** Sonar's virtual mic can report times days off, and Klipp placed its sound by them. Klipp now times such a device by when its sound arrives.

### Testing

- **Fortnite on any settings:** say what your resolution, HUD scale and language are, and whether clips were right, missing or wrong. Klipp's log shows what it read ("fortnite: …").
- **Fortnite on the default HUD:** was every elimination clipped? Did anything pause that shouldn't have?
- **Say "Klipp that" in a real match,** after teaching it. Did it miss you, and did it save anything you didn't ask for?
