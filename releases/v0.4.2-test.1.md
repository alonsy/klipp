### New

- **Minecraft auto-clips (Java Edition).** Klipp reads the game's own log and clips your PvP kills, multi-kills, and goals and challenges like Free the End. Every advancement and deaths have switches too, both off to start with. Klipp only reads a text file the game writes anyway, so nothing touches the game. The switches are at Settings → Auto-clips → Minecraft. Made by Alp ([AlpK47](https://github.com/AlpK47)), Klipp's first outside contributor. Thank you, Alp!
- **Clips you save with the key in Minecraft go to a Minecraft folder,** not to Desktop.

### Good to know

- Java Edition from the official launcher. Bedrock (Minecraft for Windows) keeps no such log, so it isn't covered. Other launchers and clients may keep their log elsewhere or name their window differently, and may not be picked up yet.
- The game doesn't write mob kills to its log, so kills are player kills only.
- Servers that rewrite chat, and games set to another language than English, may not match.
- With manual clipping off, Klipp records while a Minecraft window is open. Other Java programs don't count.

### Testing

- **Kill another player, or reach a goal or challenge.** Is there a clip in a Minecraft folder, and does it start and end where it should?
- **Forge or Fabric:** does it work with your mods?
- **Play past midnight:** are moments after midnight still clipped?
- **Press your save key in Minecraft.** Does the clip go to the Minecraft folder?
