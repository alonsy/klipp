**Test build.** For testers: Klipp installs this one by itself between games, or offers it on older test builds. Otherwise download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.4.0-test.2/Klipp-Setup.exe) and run it over your Klipp. Not a normal release yet; most people should stay on [the latest release](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/latest).

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New

- **Slow motion in exports.** Set the speed in the editor, from 0.25x to 2x, and Export to share and Export high quality come out at that speed, with the sound slowed the way you hear it there. Export panel: turn it off to export at normal speed. A trimmed copy stays at normal speed. Clips recorded at 120 fps look smoothest slowed down.
- **Name your tracks and pick their devices with Sonar.** Settings → Audio now lets you rename each track and choose its device while Sonar's tracks are on.

### Fixed

- **Without voices follows the device.** New clips know which tracks carry voices, so renaming Chat doesn't keep it in your export.

### Testing

- **Export a clip at 0.5x and 0.25x:** do the picture and sound stay together, and does it fit the size limit?
- **With Sonar:** rename a track and change a device. Do your next clips use them?
- **On AMD or Intel graphics:** do exports work at all?
