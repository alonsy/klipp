**Download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.2.1/Klipp-Setup.exe), run it, done.** Already have Klipp? It offers this update itself.

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Signed builds are coming.

### New

- **Pick the clips folder in a folder window**, like in any other program, and move your clips there in one go. They keep their trims, favorites and thumbnails, even across drives.
- **Hear the save sound while you set it up.** Setup asks whether you want the sound and how loud, and ▶ plays it on the device you chose. Settings has ▶ too.
- **Updates show up in the tray menu too**, so you hear about them even if you rarely open the Klipp window.
- **Copy details** in Settings → About, and in setup when a PC can't record: your PC, settings and recent log, ready to paste into a message or a GitHub issue.
- **What's new**, in Settings → About. It's there when you want it; Klipp never opens it by itself.
- **A warning before your clips drive fills up.**

### Changed

- **Klipp plays its own save and error sounds only.** Choosing your own WAV files is gone; if you had set one, the built-in sound plays instead.

### Fixed

- Going back from a clip returns to the same place in the library.
- The first letter of a clip card's text is no longer cut off on scaled displays.
- The update card now says what updating does: recording restarts and the replay buffer is cleared. While CS2 is running it suggests waiting until after your match.
- On laptops whose screen runs on the built-in graphics, setup now explains why it can't record yet. The next update makes it record.
