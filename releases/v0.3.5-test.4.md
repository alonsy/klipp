**Test build.** For testers: if you have 0.3.5-test.3, Klipp installs this one by itself between games; with test.1 or test.2, Klipp offers it. Otherwise download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.3.5-test.4/Klipp-Setup.exe) and run it over your Klipp. Not a normal release yet; most people should stay on [the latest release](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/latest).

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### New

- **Performance mode.** In Settings → Video, or right-click Klipp's icon next to the clock → Performance mode. Off by default.
  - **Performance:** while a game keeps your graphics card busy, Klipp records at a lower bitrate, then at a lower frame rate (half, then a third, never under 20 fps), and goes back up when the card has room again. Recording keeps running through every step, so nothing in the replay buffer is lost.
  - **Performance+:** the same, and Klipp records the lightest way your PC allows: the new Light capture and the encoder's fastest setting.
- **Light capture method.** Settings → Video → Capture method → Light. Klipp hands Windows' own picture of the screen straight to the encoder instead of copying it first, and takes fewer pictures. In a test it halved Klipp's drawing work on the graphics card. Meant for weaker graphics cards and laptops.

### Testing

- **Turn Performance mode on and play.** Does the game run smoother, and do clips still look right? Klipp writes each step to its log ("performance: …"), so say roughly when it felt different.
- On AMD graphics cards, Performance mode only lowers the frame rate: AMD's encoder keeps its bitrate while recording.
