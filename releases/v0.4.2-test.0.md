**Test build.** For testers: Klipp installs this one by itself between games, or offers it on older test builds. Otherwise download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.4.2-test.0/Klipp-Setup.exe) and run it over your Klipp. Not a normal release; most people should stay on [the latest release](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/latest).

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

This is Klipp 0.4.1, the release for everyone, as a test build: the same program on the test channel, so the next test builds start from here.

### Fixed

- **Sign in opens your browser.** In 0.4.1-test.0, pressing Sign in at Settings → Account opened your Documents folder instead of the klipp.dev page, and so did "Open the page again". Both now open the page in your browser.
