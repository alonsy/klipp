**Download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.3.5/Klipp-Setup.exe), run it, done.** Already have Klipp? It offers this update itself.

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Signed builds are coming.

### New

- **A new look.** Klipp is calmer and quicker to read: fewer words and buttons, and smooth movement between pages.
- **League of Legends auto-clips.** Multi-kills, first blood, steals and team aces become clips by themselves. Klipp asks the game's own local data while you play, so there's nothing to install.
- **Rocket League auto-clips.** Goals, epic saves and demolitions become clips. Klipp turns on Psyonix's Stats API in `TAStatsAPI.ini`, so **restart Rocket League once** after updating.
- **Klipp tests your PC.** It records a few seconds at several settings and shows what Klipp will take of your RAM, graphics card and CPU, and how big clips get. It shows once after this update, and Settings → Video can run it again.
- **Video presets.** Performance+, Performance, Normal and High, picked for your PC, or Custom.
- **Performance mode.** While a game keeps your graphics card busy, Klipp records lighter and goes back up when the card has room. Recording never stops. Off by default.
- **Squeeze space.** Settings → Storage: make clips smaller by re-encoding them on the graphics card. Each level says how much space it frees and how much quality it gives up. It replaces the clips, so it asks first.
- **Delete old clips by themselves.** By age or by total size. Only clips Klipp saved, never favorites. Off unless you turn it on.
- **Manual clipping can be off.** Then Klipp records only while a game it auto-clips is running.
- **Updates install by themselves** when you're not playing, or when Klipp starts. Settings → Advanced turns this off.

### Fixed

- **Smoother clips in demanding games.** Encoding has its own thread now, so a busy graphics card no longer holds up the next frame.
- **Full frame rate on fast screens.** 144 and 165 Hz screens no longer give choppy clips.
- **Exports work on AMD and Intel graphics.** Exporting to share or in high quality failed on any PC without an NVIDIA card.
- **AMD graphics cards keep up in games.**
- **Klipp checks your video settings after an update** and picks a lighter one if yours can't run smoothly. It tells you when it does.
- **Updates can't get stuck on a running Klipp,** and they log each step.

### Good to know

- Update checks now go through klipp.dev. Klipp sends the version you have and a random install number, nothing else, so the developer can see how many copies are in use. Settings → Advanced can turn checks off.
