**Download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.4.0/Klipp-Setup.exe), run it, done.** Already have Klipp? It offers this update itself.

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Signed builds are coming.

### New

- **Share a clip as a link.** Sign in at Settings → Account (with Steam or Discord, on klipp.dev). Then open a clip, press Export and **Share as a link**: Klipp uploads it to your Klipp account and copies a link that plays right in Discord. Only people with the link can watch it until you make it public. A free account holds 2 GB, and its shared videos start with a small Klipp tab for a moment.
- **Your clips on klipp.dev.** What you shared is in your library there, with a profile, a feed, likes, comments and follows. Clips get there from Klipp on your PC and no other way.
- **The app stays free and works without an account,** exactly as before. Nothing is uploaded unless you share it.
- **Fortnite auto-clips.** Eliminations, knocks and Victory Royales become clips by themselves. Fortnite shares nothing while a match is on, so Klipp reads your eliminations counter off the screen it records anyway; nothing touches the game. It works at 16:9, fullscreen, with the default HUD. Other screens and HUD settings get few or no clips so far. Settings → Auto-clips has its switch.
- **It stops itself if it reads wrong.** If clips come too fast, or the counter does something it can't, Fortnite's auto-clips pause for 5 minutes, and until you leave Fortnite if it happens again. Hover over Klipp's icon next to the clock to see it.
- **Say "Klipp that".** Saves a clip by voice. Off until you turn it on in Settings → Recording, where you can teach Klipp how you say it. It listens on your PC only; nothing is kept and nothing is sent.
- **Content Creator Mode (experimental).** Records your camera with every clip, and turns clips into shorts.
- **Slow motion.** Set the speed in the editor, from 0.25x to 2x, and exports come out at that speed, with the sound slowed the way you hear it there.
- **Name your tracks and pick their devices with Sonar.** Settings → Audio lets you rename each track and choose its device while Sonar's tracks are on.

### Fixed

- **The save key works in League of Legends matches.** Some games keep other apps' shortcut keys to themselves while they're in front. Klipp now also listens for the key another way and saves even when Windows doesn't pass it on. It only listens: no key is blocked or slowed down.
- **A microphone that recorded silence.** Sonar's virtual mic can report times days off, and Klipp placed its sound by them. Klipp now times such a device by when its sound arrives.
- **Without voices follows the device.** New clips know which tracks carry voices, so renaming Chat doesn't keep it in your export.

### Good to know

- Paid plans with more space, auto-backup and higher quality are on [klipp.dev/pricing](https://klipp.dev/pricing) as coming soon. Nothing is sold yet.
- With an account, Klipp sends what you share: the video, its title, game and moments, and a thumbnail. The sign-in is kept in Windows Credential Manager, and Settings → Account signs this PC out. The [privacy policy](https://klipp.dev/legal/privacy) has the rest.
