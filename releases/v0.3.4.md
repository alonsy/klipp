> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Signed builds are coming.

### Fixed

- **Smooth clips while a game works your graphics card hard.** On some PCs a demanding game left Klipp only a few frames a second, so clips played like a slideshow. Klipp's work on the graphics card now goes ahead of other apps, its video memory stays in place when a game fills the card, and frames wait their turn instead of being dropped. If the card still can't keep up, Klipp records at 60 fps instead of 120 for the rest of the session, and the sidebar says so.
- **Klipp no longer stops recording after a video hiccup.** If the graphics driver got stuck, restarting the video could freeze Klipp until the PC restarted: no clips, and the save key did nothing. Klipp now leaves the stuck part behind and records again within seconds.
- Fewer needless restarts of the screen capture while a game keeps the graphics card busy.
