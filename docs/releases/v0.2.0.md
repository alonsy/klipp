**Download [Klipp-Setup.exe](https://github.com/alonsy/klipp/releases/download/v0.2.0/Klipp-Setup.exe), run it, done.** It installs for your user (no admin) and opens setup.

> Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Signed builds are coming.

### New

- **One-click install.** No pages to click through: setup opens Klipp, which checks your PC and asks a few questions.
- **Setup that knows your PC.** It tests your graphics card's encoders at your screen's size, checks memory and storage, finds your microphone and audio devices (including SteelSeries Sonar's separate tracks), and preselects the best video settings.
- **Updates in one click.** Klipp tells you when a new version is ready; updating takes seconds and recording carries on.
- **"A clip is arriving."** Saved clips show up in the library while they're being written.
- **Any NVIDIA graphics card.** Cards that can't record AV1 switch to H.264 by themselves.
- **1440p recording**, for 4K screens.

### Fixed

- Audio and the save sound follow your Windows default devices unless you pick others.
- Exports for sharing come out under the size limit on the first try, even for short clips.
